# Product photographs

This folder is intentionally empty of images.

Until a real photograph exists, each product shows a **ghost frame** — a neutral
4:5 panel with its monoline zodiac or rudraksha mark and the caption
"Photography in progress". That is deliberate: an honest placeholder reads
better than a fabricated product shot.

## Adding real photos

Drop a JPG in here named after the product id. It appears immediately —
no code changes, no config to edit.

| Filename | Product |
|---|---|
| `bracelet-aries.jpg` … `bracelet-pisces.jpg` | the 12 zodiac bracelets |
| `rudraksha-1-mukhi.jpg` … `rudraksha-14-mukhi.jpg` | the 14 Rudraksha |
| `rudraksha-mala-108.jpg` | 108 bead mala |
| `rudraksha-siddha-mala.jpg` | Siddha mala |
| `combo-rudraksha-tiger-eye.jpg` | Rudraksha + Tiger Eye |
| `combo-seven-chakra.jpg` | Seven Chakra bracelet |

The full list of ids is in `assets/js/data.js`.

## Photo guidance

- **4:5 portrait** (e.g. 1200 × 1500). The frame crops to 4:5 — anything else
  gets trimmed top and bottom.
- Plain, undistracting background. Warm neutrals sit best against the site's paper tone.
- Keep the piece centred with room around it; the card has no border to hold it.
- No drop shadows baked into the image — the design uses none.
